01

Investment thesis

The operating inflection is real. The valuation margin of safety is not.

Great company; demanding entry point.

Data Center reached 58% of Q2 revenue and grew 107% year over year as EPYC and Instinct scaled together. Management now points to roughly $70B of Data Center revenue in 2027, more than double the current run rate. That opportunity is unusually large—but at 36× 2027 non-GAAP earnings and roughly 10× 2027 sales, much of it is already capitalized.

  • Quality is improving: H1 2026 free cash flow doubled to $4.12B, net cash approached $9.9B, and non-GAAP operating margin reached 25.9%.
  • The moat is widening: EPYC server share rose, Helios added rack-scale capability, and OpenAI, Meta and Anthropic became anchor customers and software collaborators.
  • Expectations are extreme: our reverse DCF implies about 34% annual FCF growth for a decade. Fully vested OpenAI and Meta warrants could add up to 320M shares.

04

Product scorecard

AMD is strongest where compute density, memory capacity and open systems matter. Software ecosystem depth remains the decisive gap versus NVIDIA.

EPYC
Server CPU share machine. Q2 server processor share reached 34.5%, from 27.3% a year earlier; on an EPYC-vs-Xeon-SP basis, AMD reached 46.4%. Venice brings Zen 6, TSMC N2 and up to 256 cores.
STRONG
Instinct
Credible challenger, ecosystem discount. MI455X pairs 432GB HBM4 with 19.6TB/s bandwidth; Helios integrates 72 GPUs. AMD claims up to 30% more inference tokens per dollar, but this is not independent benchmarking. CUDA remains the default software moat.
RISING
Ryzen
Share gains in a pressured market. AMD crossed 30% of client x86 share in Q2; mobile share approached 29% and mobile revenue set a record. PC memory cost pressure and a 33% YoY desktop shipment decline cloud H2.
STRONG
Radeon
Good products, weak distribution power. Discrete add-in-board share remains roughly 8% versus NVIDIA near 90%. RX 9070 XT is competitive on value and availability, but scale and ecosystem remain limited.
WEAK
Versal
High-margin recovery underway. Embedded grew 19% in Q2 after a multi-year inventory digestion. Versal Premium Gen 2 adds PCIe 6.0/CXL 3.1; Memory-on-Package variants target smaller board footprints.
RECOVERING

MI400 / Helios advantage

432GB HBM4 per MI455X versus a rumored ~288GB for NVIDIA Rubin; roughly similar memory bandwidth. AMD says its system offers 1.5× memory capacity and scale-out bandwidth. Rubin comparison figures remain rumor, and the AMD claims are not independently verified.

Anchor demand

OpenAI and Meta each committed to up to 6GW; Anthropic to up to 2GW. Microsoft is deploying Helios on Azure. These wins validate price/performance and co-develop the open stack, but also concentrate the 2027 thesis in a few counterparties.

05

Moat versus the field

AMD’s advantage is architectural flexibility and a credible open alternative—not outright ecosystem leadership.

ChipletsZen’s modular architecture now extends to accelerators and rack-scale systems.
x86 duopolyThe only scaled merchant server-CPU alternative to Intel.
Open stackROCm, Ethernet and UALink lower customer lock-in.
TSMC accessVenice is the first HPC product on N2; capacity allocation is strategic.
Systems skillZT Systems adds rack design and customer enablement.

NVIDIA

70–95% AI GPU share plus CUDA, NVLink, InfiniBand and Spectrum-X. Rubin keeps annual-cadence pressure high. The benchmark AMD must beat.

Custom silicon

Broadcom holds an estimated 60–80% of custom AI ASICs and a $73B backlog; AWS Trainium, Google TPU, Meta MTIA and Microsoft Maia narrow the merchant-GPU pool.

Intel + Arm

EPYC is taking share from Xeon, but Intel remains entrenched. Arm reached a record 13.6% server share in Q2 and is the longer-horizon architecture threat.

08

What breaks the thesis

The stock’s duration, concentration and execution demands matter as much as the product roadmap.

01
CUDA and NVIDIA’s full stack

ROCm adoption may improve without closing the ecosystem gap fast enough.

02
Custom silicon substitution

TPU, Trainium, MTIA and Maia can take workloads the merchant-GPU thesis assumes.

03
Warrant dilution

OpenAI and Meta warrants could add up to 320M shares, about 19.6% of basic shares if fully vested.

04
China controls

MI308 restrictions drove ~$440M of 2025 charges. 2026 shipment volumes remain undisclosed.

05
Customer concentration

The $70B 2027 Data Center guide leans heavily on OpenAI, Meta and Anthropic ramps.

06
Supply and mix

Wafers, HBM and packaging are tight; rack-scale growth may reduce gross-margin percentage.
