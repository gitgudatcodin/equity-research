01 / Thesis

A credible inflection, not yet a proven compounder

What the market may be missing

  • Q2 2026 revenue rose 51% year over year to $198.8M; adjusted operating profit rose 169% to $62.6M.
  • Customer assets reached $28.5B, up 79%, as net deposits remained positive.
  • Self-clearing, prediction markets, Premium and a 15-market footprint create additional monetization paths.
  • Approximately $1.85B of reported net cash provides balance-sheet support.

What could break the thesis

  • Order-flow rebates were 53% of 2025 revenue; the EU PFOF ban already constrains overseas economics.
  • Switching costs are low and much larger rivals can match price, product and promotions.
  • Q1 showed how quickly marketing spend can compress margins.
  • Supervoting control, China-related scrutiny and SPAC warrants justify a governance discount.

05 / Moat

Distribution edge, limited lock-in

2/5
Narrow / emerging

Execution, not structure

Webull’s strongest differentiators are its licensed international footprint, broad active-trader toolkit and multi-asset app. Yet customers can transfer brokers, price competition is intense, and Robinhood, Schwab, IBKR and Futu operate at much greater scale. Self-clearing could improve unit economics, but management said institutional progress has been slower than expected.

Real edge

Fifteen-market footprint, strong free tooling, zero-fee equity options and integrated crypto, futures and events.

Emerging edge

Self-clearing, Vega AI, Premium and correspondent-clearing revenue are promising, but not yet proven at scale.

Weak defense

Low switching costs, promotional customer acquisition and limited diversification versus larger peers.

Why no conventional DCF?

Reported 2025 free cash flow was $561.5M, but brokerage cash-flow statements can be dominated by customer and clearing movements. Without a verified normalized owner-earnings bridge, a DCF would create false precision.

Reverse check

At $8.25, the shares trade at 27.5× 2027E EPS and roughly 2.2× 2027E EV/sales after subtracting reported net cash. The market is pricing meaningful growth, but not peer-like durability.

08 / Risks

The discount is not accidental

01

PFOF regulation

53% of 2025 revenue came from order-flow rebates. PFOF has been banned EU-wide since June 30, 2026; US reforms could compress economics.

02

Governance & scrutiny

Founder Anquan Wang controls all supervoting Class B shares. US authorities have scrutinized the company’s connections to China.

03

SPAC overhang

BULLW and BULLZ warrants remain outstanding; more than 455M shares were released from lockup in October 2025. Fully diluted share count was not verified.

04

Cyclical economics

Trading revenue depends on volume and volatility, while 27% of 2025 revenue was interest-related and exposed to rates.

05

Cost discipline

Adjusted operating expenses rose roughly 62–64% in Q1/Q4 periods as Webull spent on performance marketing and expansion.

06

Execution

International, institutional, crypto and event-contract products widen opportunity—but also increase licensing, compliance and integration complexity.

Final recommendationHold
Webull is improving faster than its reputation, but the present price already discounts a meaningful portion of the near-term earnings ramp.

The base-case value of $9.10 offers only about 10% upside—insufficient compensation for regulatory concentration, governance and dilution risk. A sustainably high-20s operating margin, clearer non-PFOF monetization or a wider margin of safety would support a more constructive rating. This is a high-volatility, execution-dependent equity—not a core-quality compounder today.
